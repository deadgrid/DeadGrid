FAQ

What is DeadGrid?
A browser zombie survival shooter on BNB Chain, plus a 3,000-supply NFT collection. Playing is free and needs no wallet — minting is optional.

How do I mint?
Two lanes. With a Binance Agent ID: first mint FREE, extra mints 0.001 BNB. Without an Agent ID: 0.001 BNB per mint. Every mint is random — 30 types, 100 of each.

What does the NFT do?
Holders get +1 life every game run — connect your wallet on the game start screen. You can also sell it on the marketplace.

How does the marketplace work?
Fixed price, no auctions. Your NFT is held in escrow while listed. The buyer pays the exact price, you keep 97.5%, the project takes a 2.5% fee. You can cancel your listing anytime.

How does the leaderboard work?
After a game over, save your score free (gas only). Top 10 per day (UTC), one best score per wallet per day, name up to 16 characters.

What are the BNB coin drops?
Zombies drop in-game BNB coins worth 0.000001 BNB each. The total is an in-game tally saved on your device — it is NOT a payout to your wallet.

Is there a token?
Not yet. A token is planned only after the first marketplace sale. There is no token to buy right now — anything claiming to be the DeadGrid token is not ours.

Which contracts?
NFT 0x3dB8…C3eF · Scores 0x4264…06C8 · Marketplace 0x0A41…ebc — all on BNB Chain, all verified. Full addresses are in the WHAT YOU MINT card above and on BscScan.

Do I need an Agent ID to play?
No. Playing is free and needs no wallet. An Agent ID only matters for the free first mint.
