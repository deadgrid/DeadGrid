## Marketplace

- List: list(tokenId, priceWei). The NFT is escrowed; approve the marketplace first if needed.
- Buy: buy(listingId) with exact price as value. Seller keeps 97.5%, 2.5% fee to owner.
- Cancel: cancel(listingId), seller only.

## Leaderboard
